## 1. Executive Summary

### 1.1 The Market Opportunity
India is the world's second-largest crude steel producer and among the top importers of metallurgical dry bulk commodities. Public Sector Undertakings (**PSUs**) such as **SAIL, NTPC, RINL, and NMDC**, alongside private steel, cement, and power conglomerates, import over **160+ Million Metric Tonnes (MMT)** of dry bulk raw materials annually. 

The addressable annual ocean freight expenditure of Indian PSUs exceeds **₹5,000 Crore**, yet chartering operations remain heavily reliant on legacy spot tenders, fragmented broker networks, manual spreadsheet calculations, and post-fixture demurrage penalties ($15,000 – $35,000/day).

### 1.2 Our Solution & Mission
**JAL TARANG (जल तरंग)** is an AI-powered managed maritime chartering platform that combines **deep-tech algorithmic automation**, **real-time maritime telemetry**, and **human domain validation**. 

By delivering predictive freight rate curves (up to 180-day horizons), physical vessel-port compatibility vetting, Markowitz-optimized COA/Spot hedging, and automated Transchart tender generation, Jal Tarang allows Indian commodity importers to achieve **5% – 10% direct freight savings** without maintaining capital-intensive in-house shipping departments.

## 7. Unit Economics & Operational Breakeven

The unit economics of Jal Tarang demonstrate high operating leverage typical of managed marketplace platforms:

### 7.1 Single Fixture Unit Economics

| Income / Expense Element | Amount (₹) | % of Fixture Revenue | Notes |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Average Commission Revenue** | ₹2,50,000 | 86.2% | Based on standard mid-size Supramax/Panamax charter |
| **Documentation & Vetting Fee** | ₹40,000 | 13.8% | Fixed administrative, vetting & Transchart pack fee |
| **Total Revenue per Transaction ($R$)** | **₹2,90,000** | **100.0%** | Average blended fixture realization |
| Direct Broker / Expert Vetting Cost | (₹1,10,000) | 37.9% | Partner broker share & external surveyor fees |
| Data & Cloud Compute Allocation | (₹40,000) | 13.8% | API calls, AIS telemetry & server infrastructure |
| **Total Direct Costs ($C$)** | **(₹1,50,000)** | **51.7%** | Variable cost per transaction |
| **Gross Profit per Transaction ($GP$)** | **₹1,40,000** | **48.3%** | Net margin generated per completed booking |

### 7.2 Operational Breakeven Analysis

$$\text{Contribution Margin per Transaction} = R - C = ₹2,90,000 - ₹1,50,000 = ₹1,40,000$$

$$\text{Annual Fixed Operating Overheads} = ₹35,00,000 \quad \text{(Engineering, legal, base team, admin)}$$

$$\text{Operational Breakeven Volume} = \frac{\text{Annual Fixed Expenses}}{\text{Contribution Margin}} = \frac{₹35,00,000}{₹1,40,000} = \mathbf{25\text{ Transactions / Year}}$$

> [!TIP]
> **Extremely Low Breakeven Barrier:** Jal Tarang achieves full operational cash-flow breakeven with just **25 completed fixtures per year** (averaging ~2 fixtures per month). This provides substantial safety margin against market downturns.

## 10. Risk Assessment & Comprehensive Mitigation

| Identified Risk | Risk Severity | Potential Impact | Jal Tarang Defensive Strategy |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Shipbroker Resistance** | Moderate | Reluctance of traditional brokers to cooperate | **70:30 revenue-share model**; position Jal Tarang as a broker enablement copilot rather than competitor. |
| **High Initial Data Licensing Costs** | Moderate | High initial data vendor subscription fees | **Public open-data first** (Open-Meteo, AISStream, World Bank, FRED, ECB) before upgrading to licensed Baltic Exchange APIs. |
| **AI Prediction Variance / Volatility** | High | Inaccurate rate forecasting during black swan events | **Monotonic non-crossing confidence envelopes (P10/P50/P90)** paired with mandatory human Master Mariner sign-off. |
| **Sluggish PSU Procurement Adoption** | High | Long tender cycles and bureaucratic resistance | **Target mid-size private commodity traders first** to build cash flow while running institutional PSU pilots. |
| **ERP / Security Integration Hurdles** | Moderate | Delays in connecting to legacy enterprise systems | **Modular REST APIs & CSV/PDF tender exports** that require zero direct legacy code modification. |
